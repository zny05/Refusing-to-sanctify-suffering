# Nano Banana 2 Character Prompt — 小夏（女儿）— 平静依赖

## English Painting Description

Xiao Xia (daughter), 26 years old. Long dark hair in low ponytail, slender build, fair skin. She sits upright in a lightweight manual wheelchair in a modest apartment. Hands resting on lap or wheelchair armrests. Gaze directed slightly to the side, eyes soft, a quiet trusting stillness. She wears a pale blue or cream loose-fitting top and soft trousers. Soft natural morning or afternoon light, diffused through a window. Gentle shadows, low contrast. Editorial comic style, soft warm palette, clean linework. The mood conveys quiet companionship, everyday calm, mutual understanding between father and daughter. Dependence shown through stillness, not through distress.
