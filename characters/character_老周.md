## English Painting Description

Old Zhou (father), 56 years old. A working-class East Asian man with a weathered face, deep-set eyes, and short cropped graying hair. Lean but stocky build with visible muscle tension in arms and shoulders from years of physical labor. Posture slightly hunched forward, suggesting chronic lower back discomfort. He wears a dark blue work jacket (deep navy, long-sleeved, slightly worn at the elbows), gray cotton trousers, and simple dark slippers at home. Domestic morning scene in a modest apartment. Warm yellow morning light from a small window. A bed with thin bedding and a lightweight manual wheelchair positioned at its foot. He stands with a slight forward lean, one hand resting on his lower back. Editorial comic style, clean linework, warm muted palette, dignified working-class character design. He must never be depicted as a villain or a helpless victim. He is a capable man limited by circumstances and cultural expectations.
