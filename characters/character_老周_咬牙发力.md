# Nano Banana 2 Character Prompt — 老周（父亲）— 咬牙发力

## English Painting Description

Old Zhou (father), 56 years old. Same base description: dark blue work jacket, gray cotton trousers, stocky build with chronic back issues. The moment of lifting his daughter from bed to wheelchair. Peak physical exertion. Bending at the waist with arms extended under his daughter's armpits, both hands gripping fabric. Legs slightly bent. Entire torso and arm muscles visibly tensed. Veins prominent on forearms and neck. Face contorted with effort, teeth clenched showing, brow deeply furrowed. Sweat beginning at temples. Dark blue work jacket with sleeves slightly rolled up, gray cotton trousers. Directional warm light from side window, casting strong shadows on muscle tension lines. Editorial comic style, dramatic lighting, warm palette with high contrast shadows. This is the key image of Act I. It must convey strain without glorifying it. Raw physical effort, silent determination, pain masked by willpower.
