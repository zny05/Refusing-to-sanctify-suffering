# Nano Banana 2 Character Prompt — 我（叙述者）

## English Painting Description

The narrator ("我" / "I"). An observer, likely a medical professional or someone close to the situation, witnessing events from a slight distance. This character appears minimally in the visual narrative — mostly as voiceover/旁白 text — to avoid stealing focus from Old Zhou and Xiao Xia. When depicted: a figure in casual everyday clothing (button-up shirt or simple jacket), seen from behind, in profile, or as a partial figure in a doorway or hallway. The figure carries an uncomfortable, reflective expression — a tightness in the jaw, a furrowed brow — the look of someone who knows something should change but hasn't yet. Mood: witness, not participant. Their discomfort mirrors the audience's. Note: this character is NARRATIVE FUNCTION ONLY. 95% of scenes will use 旁白 text instead of showing this character visually.
