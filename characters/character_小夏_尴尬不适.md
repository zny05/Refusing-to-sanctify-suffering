# Nano Banana 2 Character Prompt — 小夏（女儿）— 尴尬不适

## English Painting Description

Xiao Xia (daughter), 26 years old. Long dark hair in low ponytail, slender build, fair skin. She is being lifted by her father from bed to wheelchair — her body completely limp and dependent, arms hanging at her sides, legs dangling off the bed. Her eyes are briefly averted from her father's face. Slight flush on her cheeks, a small tense line at the corner of her mouth. She wears a pale blue loose top and trousers. Morning bedroom scene with warm but slightly muted morning light, shadows emphasizing the vulnerability of the moment. Editorial comic style, muted warm palette, clean linework. This expression is subtle, private, brief — NOT dramatic or pitiful. It conveys unspoken guilt about being a physical burden, dignity aching beneath compliance.
